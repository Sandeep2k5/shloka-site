Shloka Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026

Shloka is an app for reading the Bhagavad Gita. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. In short: there is no account, your reading stays on your phone, and the only data that leaves your phone is what Google's advertising service collects to show ads.

What stays on your phone

The app saves these on your device only. We never receive them:

  • your chosen language and reminder time;
  • the verses you save, where you stopped reading, and the days you read (for your streak);
  • whether you have Premium or a day without ads.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, deletes all of it.

Daily verse notifications are scheduled on your phone. Recitation uses your phone's own text-to-speech engine. Verse images you share are made on your phone and go only where you choose to send them.

Advertising

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google's advertising software in the app collects:

  • your device's IP address, which can indicate your general location;
  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID;
  • how you interact with ads (for example, views and taps);
  • diagnostic information about the app's and the ad software's performance.

This data is sent to Google, encrypted in transit, and handled under Google's Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

We do not tell Google anything about your beliefs, what you read, or which verses you save, and ads are never targeted by religion.

Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later under Menu, About, "Privacy choices for ads". On any Android phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, under Privacy, Ads.

Shloka Premium removes all ads. With Premium, the app does not request ads.

Purchases

Premium is bought through Google Play. Google handles payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether you own Premium. Google Play's handling of purchases is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

  • There is no account, sign-up or login.
  • We run no servers that receive your data, and we use no analytics of our own.
  • We do not sell your information.
  • The app does not use your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

Children

Shloka is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
